# Legal Boundary

The project uses an **extractor-first archival architecture**. Read
`docs/legal/ASSET_POLICY.md` — it is the authority; this file is the
working summary.

## Commit directly

Complete named inventories (spells, items, monsters, characters,
commands, formations, maps, shops, treasures, abilities, music, sound
effects), IDs, **names and short labels**, statistics, flags, formulas,
mechanics, addresses, pointers, layouts, dimensions, relationships,
hashes, provenance, event/AI opcode definitions, project-authored
descriptions, Go reconstruction code, extractors and decoders, tests,
and asset manifests.

## Generate locally (never commit)

ROMs, savestates, screenshots, emulator captures, raw ROM slices, and
substantial graphics/audio/dialogue/map/script material. These live
under `local_artifacts/` — the archive itself under
`local_artifacts/archive/`, regenerated by `ff6lab extract all`.

## The rule that closes the loophole

A placeholder like `"name": "see local extraction (asset policy)"` in a
permitted field is a **violation**, not compliance. Names are permitted;
withholding them makes the public inventory useless. Unresolved values
may say `unknown` — but never invent a value, and never hide a known one.

Enforced by `internal/census.TestNoPlaceholdersInPermittedNameFields`.
