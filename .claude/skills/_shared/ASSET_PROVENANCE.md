# Asset Provenance

Each asset must record:

- stable asset ID;
- asset class;
- local path;
- SHA-256;
- ROM and emulator identity;
- scenario and frame/cue;
- source memory domains and ranges;
- loading/decompression routines;
- palette/layout/sequence dependencies;
- reconstruction command;
- validation result;
- confidence.

Runtime viewer exports are classified as runtime captures until traced to their underlying representation.
