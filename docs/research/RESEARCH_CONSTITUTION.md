# Research Constitution

1. Preserve evidence before interpretation.
2. Prefer controlled experiments over narrative inference.
3. Use the smallest experiment that discriminates alternatives.
4. Keep behavior, data structure, graphics, and audio claims separately addressable.
5. No semantic name outruns its evidence.
6. Runtime state and canonical ROM representation are different things.
7. The Go implementation expresses proven behavior, not assembly-shaped speculation.
8. Every session ends with durable state.
9. Contradictions are first-class records.
10. Reproducibility outranks speed.
