# Evidence Standard

## Four confidence levels

### Confirmed
Repeated, reproducible observations discriminate the conclusion from credible alternatives.

### Strong hypothesis
Multiple independent observations support the conclusion, but one meaningful ambiguity remains.

### Tentative hypothesis
A plausible interpretation supported by limited evidence.

### Unknown
Insufficient evidence. Preserve raw observations without forcing a name.

## Required separation

Every record must distinguish:

- **Observation:** what was directly measured.
- **Interpretation:** what the observation may mean.
- **Alternatives:** other viable explanations.
- **Falsification:** what outcome would disprove or materially weaken the interpretation.
